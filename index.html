New Listing Onboarding

When a new mandate comes in, you take it from "we just signed an owner" all the way to "the listing is live and bookable on every channel." This is the most repeatable, process-driven part of your role — and the foundation of everything else.

The End-to-End Flow

Eight steps, in this order. Skipping or reordering them creates problems further down the line.

  1. Airtable Setup — Receive the listing, mark it Pre-Onboarding, and complete every mandatory field.
  2. Canva Image Setup — Build the listing's image sequence at 2048 × 1365 px.
  3. Boom Setup — Add the homeowner, create the listing, and configure every tab.
  4. PriceLabs — Connect the listing, set base and minimum price, enable sync.
  5. Airbnb — Publish from Boom, push to Airbnb, monitor connection.
  6. Booking.com — Set up via QuickConnect, configure rate plans, link to Boom.
  7. LekkerGuest — Add financials and sync.
  8. Google Travel (optional, when eligible).
Golden Rule of Onboarding

If any mandatory information is missing, stop. Do not push the listing forward, do not create workarounds, and do not "fill in something close." Flag the gap, get the answer, then continue. This is the single biggest difference between a clean onboarding and a chaotic one.

Section 2.1

Airtable & Canva

Onboarding starts before you touch a single platform. Two things have to be right first: the data in Airtable, and the image sequence in Canva. Get these wrong and the rest of the setup will be wrong too.

Step 1 — Airtable Setup

Airtable is the single source of truth for owner, mandate, and listing information. Every onboarding starts and ends here.

A Receive & Locate the Listing
  • Monitor Slack for a new listing form notification.
  • Open Airtable → Properties → Engaging view.
  • Locate the new property.
  • Change status → Pre-Onboarding.
B Complete Mandatory Fields
  • Assign the homeowner.
  • Upload the signed mandate.
  • Select Virtual Property Management (Yes / No).
  • Enter the management fee.
  • Add the Property Manager (name, email, phone).
  • Select the management solution.
  • Assign Social #.
  • Enter the security deposit and damage waiver.
  • Confirm all owner-submitted info is complete.
C Verify Critical Info
  • Property address.
  • Cleaning setup.
  • Wi-Fi details.
  • Lockbox / smart lock details.
  • Lock code.
  • Arrival instructions (codes, access, forms).
⚠ Do Not Proceed if Anything is Missing

This is where most onboarding pain comes from. A missing Wi-Fi password, a missing lock code, a missing arrival instruction — each one becomes a guest issue downstream. Pause, get the missing piece, then continue.

D Create & Share the Booking View
  • Go to the Bookings tab.
  • Duplicate the Property Report view.
  • Rename the duplicate to the property name.
  • Apply filter: Property Name contains the correct property.
  • Create a share link, copy it, and paste it into "Bookings Report Link" in Airtable.

Step 2 — Canva (Images Setup)

Photos are the single biggest driver of bookings on Airbnb and Booking.com. The order matters as much as the quality. Always work from the same template structure so the portfolio feels consistent.

A Folder Setup
  • Log into Canva.
  • Locate or create the property folder.
  • Create two subfolders: Low Res and High Res.
B Create or Locate the Design
  • Check if a design already exists for the property.
  • If not, create a new design at 2048 × 1365 px.

The Image Sequence Rule

The first five images decide whether a guest scrolls or skips. Always lead with these, in this order:

  1. Best overall shot — the single most striking image of the property.
  2. Exterior — the building or grounds in their best light.
  3. The view — what guests will actually see when they're there.
  4. Living area — the heart of the home.
  5. Main bedroom — the space they'll sleep in.

After those five, add remaining images in a logical guest-walkthrough flow:

Entrance → Living → Kitchen → Bedrooms → Bathrooms → Outdoor → Amenities → Views

C Final Review & Download
  • Confirm the order is logical and guest-focused.
  • Confirm images fill their frames correctly.
  • Download the final images.
  • Verify: correct folder, correct size, ready to upload.
Why the Sequence Matters

Airbnb and Booking.com both display the first photo as the listing's hero image in search results. If the hero isn't the strongest shot, you lose clicks before a guest even reads the title.

Section 2.2

Boom & PriceLabs

Boom is our PMS — the operational core where the listing actually lives. PriceLabs is the dynamic pricing engine that keeps daily rates competitive. Together, these two systems power every other channel.

Step 3 — Boom Setup

Boom is configured in three blocks: the homeowner, the listing itself, and the calendar. Work through them in order.

A Add the Homeowner
  • Configuration → Settings → Add User.
  • Role = Investor.
  • Fill in the homeowner's details.
  • Enable the appropriate permissions.
  • Toggle notifications: ON for reservations, OFF for payments.
  • Send the homeowner their login details.
Why Notifications Matter

Owners care about reservations — those are bookings on their property. Payment notifications, on the other hand, are noisy and confusing for them. Always: ON reservations, OFF payments.

B Create the Listing
  • Click + → New Listing.
  • Fill in: name / nickname, address, bedrooms, bathrooms, max guests, description.
  • Save.

Step 3C — Configure the Listing (Tab by Tab)

Boom has multiple tabs for a listing. Work through each one.

General
  • Title and nickname match.
  • Address and coordinates correct.
  • Check-in and check-out times set.
  • Bedrooms and beds entered.
  • All description fields completed.
Pricing & Fees
  • Security deposit.
  • Cleaning fee.
  • Damage waiver.
  • Cancellation policy.
Amenities
  • Select all relevant amenities. Don't be conservative — every box ticked helps the listing surface in filtered searches.
Images
  • Upload the Canva image sequence in the same order you saved them.
Information
  • Door codes.
  • Parking details.
  • Wi-Fi name and password.
  • Arrival and departure instructions.
Personnel
  • Assign the relevant staff (cleaners, ground operator, etc.).

Step 3D — Configuration Tab

  • Region set to the correct regional group.
  • Owner selected.
  • Owner vacation toggle ON.
  • Commission = 0.1725.
Commission — Get This Exact

The commission must be entered as 0.1725 — not 17.25, not 0.17, not 17%. Wrong commission affects every owner payout going forward.

E Calendar Setup
  • Pricing engine = PriceLabs.
  • Set the booking rules.
  • Sync with PriceLabs.
  • Block the calendar (the listing isn't live to book yet).

Step 4 — PriceLabs

PriceLabs needs three things from you: an account connection, base and minimum prices, and an active sync with Boom.

A Connect the Account
  • In PriceLabs, add or reconnect the listing.
  • Select Boom as the PMS.
  • Enable sync.
B Connect on Boom's Side
  • Settings → Integrations.
  • Add the PriceLabs email.
  • Save.
C Configure Pricing
  • Set the minimum price (the floor — the listing will never drop below this).
  • Set the base price (the engine's anchor for dynamic pricing).
  • Save and refresh.
  • Enable the sync toggle.
Base vs Minimum — The 1.33× Rule

Set the base price at roughly 1.33× the minimum (a 25–45% gap is the safe range). If they're too close, PriceLabs has no room to flex prices up or down — and the entire dynamic pricing strategy collapses. The Revenue Manager will fine-tune from there.

Where the Pricing Engine Sits

You're not setting the daily rates yourself. You're giving PriceLabs the boundaries it needs to do its job. Daily rates, seasonality, last-minute discounts, and far-out premiums are all the Revenue Manager's domain.

Section 2.3

Channel Publishing

With Boom and PriceLabs in place, the listing is ready to go live across our channels. Publish in this order: Airbnb first, then Booking.com, then LekkerGuest. Google Travel comes last and only when eligible.

Step 5 — Airbnb

Airbnb publishing happens entirely from Boom. You won't log into Airbnb directly during onboarding.

A Publish the Listing
  • Marketing → Channels.
  • Open "Unpublished Listings".
  • Click Publish.
  • Resolve any errors that come up (missing fields, invalid amenities, etc.).
B Push to Airbnb
  • Select the Airbnb channel.
  • Add the property.
  • Select the listing.
  • Set markup = 33%.
  • Click Create listing.
C Monitor
  • Status → Listed.
  • Sync → Connected.
A Note on the Airbnb Markup

The setup markup is 33%, but it's adjusted depending on listing maturity. Brand-new listings drop to 20% with a New Listing promotion to drive early bookings. After roughly three bookings, it returns to 33%. We'll cover this in detail in section 3.1.

Step 6 — Booking.com

Booking.com is set up in the BDC Extranet first, then connected to Boom. This is a bigger configuration than Airbnb because BDC has more pricing levers.

A Initial Setup (BDC Extranet)
  • Add the property.
  • Enter the full address manually.
  • Import the Airbnb link (BDC pulls some data from it).
  • Select QuickConnect as the connectivity method.
B Property Details & Rules
  • Rooms and capacity.
  • Apartment size.
  • Description (copy from Boom).
  • Host profile added.
  • Select all relevant amenities.
  • Complete house rules.
C Photos
  • Remove any images BDC imported from Airbnb.
  • Upload the Canva image sequence in the same order.
D Pricing Setup (Temporary)
  • Set a temporary rate of R8000.
  • Cancellation = 30 days.
  • Disable any extra rate plans BDC creates by default — we'll set up the proper rate plans in section 3.2.
E Channel Manager & Policies
  • Select "Connect to channel manager" → choose QuickConnect.
  • Block the calendar.
  • Set guest requirements.
  • Fees: 2.5% service fee + cleaning.
  • Damage deposit.
F Connect to Boom
  • In Boom: Marketing → Channels → Booking.com.
  • Enter the BDC property ID.
  • Match the listing.
  • Set markup = 85%.
  • Click Link & Connect.
G Final Checks
  • "Connection activated" message appears.
  • Prices are syncing from Boom to BDC.
  • Listing is visible in BDC.
Don't Skip the Order

Set up rate plans in BDC before activating promotions. Promotions attach themselves to existing rate plans — if you change rate plans later, promotions don't carry over and you'll have to redo them. Section 3.2 covers the proper rate plan structure.

Step 7 — LekkerGuest

LekkerGuest is the lightest channel to set up. The bookings come through automatically once it's wired up.

A Setup & Finalize

  • Save.
  • Sync bookings.

A Verify Requirements
  • Property type valid.

B Connect
  • Boom → Channels → Google.

Section 3.1

Airbnb Optimization

Airbnb is a content-driven platform. Photos and copy do most of the selling work — the pricing levers are simpler than Booking.com. Get the basics right and the listing performs.

Confirm the Boom Connection

Before anything else, in Boom: Marketing → Channels → Airbnb. Confirm the listing is connected. If it isn't, no other change you make here will sync.

The Five Description Fields

Airbnb gives you five distinct content fields. Each one has a job. Filling them all properly is one of the highest-impact things you can do for a listing's ranking.

FieldPurposeLimit
Listing descriptionThe first thing guests see. Lead with the strongest concrete fact.500 chars
Your propertyDetailed property description, structured by section.—
Guest accessExactly what guests will have access to.—
Interaction with guestsHow communication will be handled during the stay.—

The Non-Negotiables

🛡

Cancellation Policy: Firm

Always set to Firm. This balances guest flexibility with protection against last-minute cancellations.

⚡

Instant Book: ON

Always ON. Instant Book listings rank higher and convert better than request-only listings.

Markup Strategy

The Airbnb markup depends on whether the listing is brand new or already active.

Listing StateMarkupWhy
NEW listing20%Drive early bookings; pair with the New Listing promotion.
Already active33%Standard channel margin once the listing has booking history.
When Does "New" Become "Active"?

Once three bookings have happened. That's the trigger to move from 20% markup to 33% and to switch the discount strategy from "New Listing promotion only" to the full early-bird + last-minute combination below.

Promotions & Discounts

For NEW listings (until the third booking lands)

  • Activate the New Listing promotion at 20%.
  • Do not apply the 33% markup yet — keep markup at 20% until the third booking.
  • The goal: get bookings on the board fast. ADR can come later.

For ACTIVE listings (3+ bookings)

Use the structured discount pair below — together they give a guest a discount no matter when they're booking, which is exactly what we want.

  • Early-bird discount: 20% off for guests who book at least 1 month before arrival.
  • Last-minute discount: 20% off for guests booking 0–28 days before arrival.
The Always-On Discount Pattern

When you stack early-bird (1+ month out) and last-minute (0–28 days), every possible booking window has a discount applied. There's never a "dead zone" where a guest sees the full price with no incentive — and that's intentional.

Section 3.2

Booking.com Optimization

Booking.com has more pricing levers than any other channel. The wins come from understanding how rate plans, partner programmes, and promotions stack — and applying them in the right order.

Step 1 — Connectivity Provider

In the BDC Extranet → Account icon (top right) → Connectivity Provider. Always set to QuickConnect.

Step 2 — Rate Plans (Set These FIRST)

Order Matters

Set up rate plans before promotions. If you change rate plans later, the promotions you've already configured won't apply to the new ones — and you'll have to redo everything.

Every listing should have two rate plans:

  • Standard Rate (Flex 30)
  • Non-Refundable

How to add a new rate plan

In BDC Extranet: Rates & Availability → Add new rate plan → Custom (follow the steps) → at step 6, select "Set up as a new rate plan".

In Boom: Marketing → Channels → Booking.com → enter the BDC ID → Manage rates → Sync changes from Booking.com → the new rate plans appear.

Step 3 — Markups (the Matrix)

The markup depends on which promotions you can run. Three scenarios:

ScenarioNon-RefundableStandard Rate
All Promotions + Genius75%85%
All Promotions, no Genius57%67%
New listing — Targeting only21%31%
How to Pick Your Scenario

Check what's currently available for the listing. Some promotions aren't immediately available for new listings — the markup follows what you can actually run, so you don't end up undercutting yourself when the bigger promotions go live.

Step 4 — Partner Programmes

BDC offers three partner programmes. Our position on each is fixed.

Genius — JOIN if eligible

Eligibility: review score above 7.5 and at least 3 reviews.

Settings:

  • Level 1, Non-Dynamic discount.
  • All rooms selected.
  • Manage your audience: rate sharing across different channels selected.

In high season we may consider Level 2 or 3 by lifting the markup accordingly — confirm with the Revenue Manager first.

Preferred Partner — JOIN if eligible

Always join if eligible. Better placement, better visibility.

Preferred Partner PLUS — DO NOT join

Cost-benefit analysis in the South African market shows the additional commission isn't worth it. Always decline.

Step 5 — Promotions (How They Stack)

BDC offers five promotion families. They almost all stack on top of each other — the exception being Deep Deals. Here's how we run each one.

Genius (Badge)

Already covered above. Always Level 1 Non-Dynamic = 10% off for any guest with a BDC account.

Targeting

Always activate both at 10%:

  • Mobile Rate (badge) — App + mobile web platforms selected.
  • Country Rate — all countries selected.

Portfolio Deals

PromoDiscountRule
Basic Deal21%Members & newsletter subscribers only — Secret deal selected.
Last-Minute Deal21%Maximum 12 days before check-in.
Early Booker Deal21%Minimum 13 days before check-in.

Campaign Deals (Badge)

BDC runs three campaign deals through the year — Early 2026 Deal, Getaway Deal, Late Escape Deal. Functionally similar; differ by date window.

Always activate at 31%.

Deep Deals (Badge)

Deep Deals Don't Stack

Deep Deals give the biggest boost in rank and exposure, but they must be at least 30% and they do not stack with any other promotion — not even Genius. Use them surgically.

Limited-Time Deal (LTD)

Top of search list. Powerful but heavily constrained: active for only 2 days at a time, then a full 2 weeks must pass before re-activation.

Example: activate on the 1st–2nd of January → next eligible activation is the 17th of January.

Euphoric Leisure runs an LTD rotation across listings to share the boost across the portfolio without breaking the cooldown rule.

Black Friday Deal

One of the most powerful deals BDC offers. Runs once a year — late November to early December — but the bookable period extends a year into the future. Always activate Black Friday Deal.

Step 6 — Grace Period

Set the grace period to 24 hours after a booking comes in. This gives us a buffer to catch and act on issues before the reservation is locked in irrevocably.

Section 3.3

Listing Content Best Practices

Photos get the click. Content closes the booking. Strong listing copy is honest, specific, and direct — and crucially, sounds nothing like every other rental on the platform. This section is our house style.

Why This Matters

Most rental copy is interchangeable. "Thoughtfully designed," "nestled in," "perfect blend of," "whether you're here for…" — readers skim past it because every other listing says the same thing. Distinctive, concrete writing performs measurably better.

The Euphoric Leisure House Style

◆

Lead with the strongest concrete fact

Don't open with mood. Open with what makes this property different — a distance, a feature, a layout, a view.

▢

Use section headers

Living Space, Kitchen, Bedroom, Outdoor, Included. Headers help skim-readers find what they care about.

⊟

2–5 short, direct sentences per section

If a paragraph is longer than five sentences, cut it. Short prose reads as confident.

⊘

No em dashes

They're a tell of AI-written copy. Use commas, full stops, or hyphens instead.

Banned Words & Phrases

These words appear in almost every generic rental listing. They add no information. Avoid them.

  • "Thoughtfully designed" — empty filler.
  • "Seamlessly" — describes nothing concrete.
  • "Nestled in / nestled among" — every cottage is "nestled."
  • "Perfect blend of…" — meaningless.
  • "Whether you're here for X, Y, or Z…" — try-too-hard sentence opener.
  • Em dashes (—) — replace with commas or full stops.

Fee Transparency

Always disclose fees that aren't included in the nightly rate. Gate fees, conservation fees, environmental levies and similar charges are common in South African properties — disclose them as "payable on arrival", not bundled into the price. Transparent pricing protects reviews.

The 500-Character Rule

Listing Description Limit

Airbnb's Listing description field caps at 500 characters. Track your character count as you write. This is the field guests see first — make every character count.

Your Writing Checklist

Before you publish any new listing copy, run through this list:

  • Opens with a concrete fact, not a mood.
  • Has section headers (Living, Kitchen, Bedroom, Outdoor, etc.).
  • Each section is 2–5 short sentences.
  • No em dashes anywhere.
  • None of the banned words and phrases above.
  • Listing description (the 500-char field) is under 500 characters.
  • Any payable-on-arrival fees are clearly disclosed.
  • Sounds like a person wrote it, not a generic template.
